Quality services

From maintenance to major repair.

We service domestic, European, and Asian vehicles. Every appointment starts with a 32-point inspection so you can approve work with a full picture — not a sales pitch.

Diagnostics & check engine light

We read the codes, test the system, and tell you what is actually failing — then recommend only the repair that solves it.

Oil change & factory maintenance

Factory-scheduled service, filters, fluids, and the little items that keep a vehicle reliable between bigger jobs.

Brake repair & service

Pads, rotors, hydraulics, and hardware. We measure wear and explain what is safety-critical versus what can wait.

Wheel alignment

A straight steering wheel, even tire wear, and a vehicle that tracks true after suspension work or a curb strike.

A/C & heating

Texas heat is not optional. We diagnose leaks, compressors, and blend issues so the cabin stays livable.

Battery & charging system

Batteries, alternators, and starters tested under load so you are not replacing parts that were never the problem.

Electrical system repair

From no-starts to parasitic drains, we trace circuits instead of swapping modules and hoping.

Engine repair & performance

Misfires, leaks, timing, and performance issues handled by technicians who work these engines every day.

Transmission & drivetrain

Fluid service, drivability concerns, and drivetrain repairs with a clear plan before we open the case.

Steering & suspension

Clunks, wander, and worn bushings diagnosed on the alignment rack and the road — not just from a parts list.

Cooling system repair

Overheating is an engine-killer. We find leaks, failed fans, and clogged radiators before they become a tow.

Fuel system & induction

Fuel delivery, injectors, and induction service when drivability, mileage, or idle quality start to slip.
