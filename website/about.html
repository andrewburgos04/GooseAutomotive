About us

We don’t just love cars. We love people.

Goose Automotive is a family-owned repair network across Texas. We exist so drivers can walk into a shop without wondering if they are about to be taken advantage of.

The beginning

Named for Goose. Built for families.

The owner, Gustavo “Goose” Macharro, opened this company because when he was young, his mother was ripped off by an unethical mechanic. He remembered the hurt and the frustration — and he was determined to build a shop where people could bring a vehicle in with confidence.

Goose Automotive started as a family-friendly shop staffed by ethical mechanics who loved cars and hot rods. Today we love our customers and our communities just as much. More than 50,000 Texas drivers have trusted us since 2005.

What we believe

Explain first. Sell never.

Inspections before estimates

Every appointment starts with a 32-point inspection. You get a detailed look at the vehicle’s health before any wrench turns.

Priority, not pressure

Advisors help you sort what is urgent from what you can plan and budget. You approve the work. Always.

A warranty that travels

Most repairs are backed by a 3-year / 36,000-mile nationwide warranty. If it is not right, we fix it — no matter where you are in the country.

In the shop

Certified techs. Comfortable lobby.

Our mechanics are ASE-certified and manufacturer-certified, whether the job is factory maintenance or a full-scale repair. Most locations have a waiting area with Wi-Fi. Pickup and delivery are available — ask your shop when you book.

Qualified customers can use six months same as cash financing when a repair cannot wait.

The Goose standard

We believe long-term trust beats short-term profit. That is why we stay family-owned, locally operated, and stubborn about doing the job right the first time.

FAQs

Straight answers.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended to reduce wait time. Walk-ins are accepted when availability allows.

Do you offer same-day service?

Many maintenance jobs and minor repairs can be completed the same day, depending on the schedule and parts.

Can I wait at the shop?

Yes. Most locations have waiting areas available.

Do you work on European vehicles?

Yes. We service European, domestic, and Asian vehicles. Some locations specialize in European brands — West Avenue, Colorado Street, and Universal City are good starting points.

Do you offer a warranty?

Most repairs are backed by a 3-year / 36,000-mile nationwide warranty.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing options, including six months same as cash for qualified customers, are available to help manage unexpected repairs.

Do you service fleets?

Yes. We service commercial and fleet vehicles at participating locations, including government vehicles as a GSA fleet vendor.
